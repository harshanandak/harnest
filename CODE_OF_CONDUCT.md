# Code of conduct

Harnest follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Everyone who takes part in issues, pull requests, discussions, and reviews agrees to it.

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, insults, personal attacks, and publishing other people's private information are not accepted.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems privately through [GitHub private reporting](https://github.com/harshanandak/harnest/security/advisories/new) and start the title with "Conduct:". The maintainer reads every report, keeps the reporter's identity private, and replies within 7 days.

If your report concerns the maintainer, report it to GitHub instead through [Report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) or [GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/contact), so someone other than the maintainer reviews it.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, and issues that break this code, and may warn, temporarily block, or permanently ban a participant, following the Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines.
